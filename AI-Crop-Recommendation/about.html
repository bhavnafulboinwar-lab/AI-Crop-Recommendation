About The System

Smart Farming through Artificial Intelligence and Data Analytics

Our Mission

To empower farmers and agricultural enthusiasts with easy-to-use digital tools that analyze soil health and environment, recommending the highest yielding crop varieties while minimizing fertilizing costs.

Our Vision

To build a sustainable farming future powered by AI, preventing crop failures, optimizing food supply chains, and maximizing ecological resources in an era of unpredictable climate shifts.

System Objectives

Map soil NPK configurations, calibrate optimal pH, cross-reference historical crop weather tolerances, and visualize recommendations through a real-time responsive browser application.

AI-Agronomy Simulation

Dynamic mapping between soil chemical indices and plant taxonomy profiles.

System Architecture

A review of technical integration and implementation scopes.

Why AI?

Traditional crop selection relies on manual trial-and-error, which causes massive financial deficits. Machine learning simulation matches multi-dimensional thresholds in milliseconds, eliminating yield errors.

Technology Stack

Built exclusively as a static Netlify-ready system. Leverages standard HTML5, CSS3 transitions, Bootstrap 5 responsive utility grids, Vanilla JS state management, and Chart.js graphics loaders.

Future Scope

Future iterations aim to interface with real-time hardware microcontrollers (ESP32 / Arduino), connect Live weather API scrapers, and employ satellite multispectral imaging arrays.

AI Crop Recommendation

Enabling digital agronomy by predicting the best yielding crop for your soils. Designed with clean SaaS aesthetics and responsive components.
