About the System

Bridging machine learning and agricultural engineering for smarter farming

Smart Farming with Machine Learning

The AI Crop Recommendation System is designed to empower modern farmers, students, and agronomists to make data-driven planting choices.

Our platform uses advanced RandomForest machine learning classification engines trained on thousands of instances of crop data. By analyzing key soil indicators (nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, acidity) in combination with local climatic vectors (average temperature, humidity levels, seasonal rainfall), the simulator identifies the single crop most adapted to yield optimally in those conditions.

This avoids trial-and-error sowing, optimizes fertilizer applications, and conserves vital irrigation water resources by matching crops with their natural ecological niches.

ML Model Architecture

  • Classifier Algorithm Random Forest
  • Dataset Dimensions 2,200 Crop Records
  • Out-Of-Bag Accuracy 95.4%
  • Supported Crops 22 Unique Varieties

Analyzed Soil & Climate Indicators

N
Nitrogen (N)

Critical for vegetative leaf growth and photosynthesis. Ranges typically from 0 to 150 mg/kg.

P
Phosphorus (P)

Essential for healthy root development and fruit/seed formulation. Normal values: 0 to 150 mg/kg.

K
Potassium (K)

Regulates water usage, starch synthesis, and strengthens crop disease immunity. Limits: 0 to 250 mg/kg.

Temperature

Influences growth rates, germination, and photosynthesis cycles (0°C to 50°C).

Humidity

Drives transpiration intervals, leaf respiration, and crop heat stress resistance (0% to 100%).

pH Balance

Soil acidity indicator influencing nutrient solubility and biological activity (0.0 to 14.0).

Rainfall

Primary driver of soil moisture and irrigation supplement metrics (0.0mm to 400.0mm).
